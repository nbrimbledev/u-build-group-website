# U Build Group SEO audit, September 25, 2026

Scope: public Group homepage, Team and Gallery. Construction and Everett websites were not modified. Search Console reports, ranking history and real-user Core Web Vitals were not available in this audit.

## Verified before changes

All three public pages returned HTTP 200 and had one H1 and distinct search descriptions. Robots allowed crawling, sitemap was accessible, an unknown URL returned 404, and the Google ownership file was accessible. HTTPS and non-www requests redirected correctly to www.

Canonical tags, sitemap entries and organization URLs nevertheless pointed to the redirecting non-www host. Team and Gallery inherited homepage Open Graph URLs and descriptions. The team sitemap date also predated Angelique's correction.

A single Lighthouse mobile run against the production homepage scored performance 73, accessibility 100 and SEO 100. LCP was 5.4 seconds, total blocking time 40 ms and CLS 0. These are lab measurements, not field metrics or a ranking score. Basic SEO checks do not establish that Google has indexed a page or chosen its intended canonical.

## Changes

- Align canonical URLs, organization URLs, robots sitemap reference and sitemap entries with https://www.ubuildgroup.ca.
- Give Team and Gallery their own Open Graph and social descriptions and URLs.
- Include the Group's verified Instagram link in organization metadata. Avoid claiming the Construction company's social profiles as Group profiles.
- Name both active companies in the visible homepage introduction.
- Include the published gallery image URLs in the sitemap without restoring visible captions.
- Use thumbnail sources where available and lower image quality for the decorative hero reel; prioritize the actual page content over background images.
- Load the interactive map only near its viewport, with a manual load button and a no-JavaScript link.
- Preserve the Google ownership verification file.

## Remaining work

The 32 imported OneDrive photos still have camera-derived descriptions. They should receive reviewed image-specific alt text. Project names and locations must not be guessed. Visible titles remain hidden as requested.

In verified Google Search Console, submit https://www.ubuildgroup.ca/sitemap.xml and request indexing for the homepage. Monitor the chosen canonical, indexed pages and branded search impressions. These account actions have not been performed by this audit.

Automatic OneDrive synchronization still requires administrator activation. Construction project photos refresh when the Group site is built; changing the Construction site alone does not deploy the Group site.
