# Colours

The Group page uses cool grey surfaces and navy anchors. Company colours are reserved for routing and map identification. These values describe this website, not the full identity specifications of its sister companies.

| Role | Value | Code token | Current use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cool paper | `#F7F9FB` | `--paper` | Lightest page and header surface |
| Mineral grey | `#E8EDF2` | `--mineral` | Quiet supporting surfaces |
| Deep mineral | `#D8E0E8` | `--mineral-deep` | Stronger grey separation |
| Charcoal ink | `#111C26` | `--ink` | Main text |
| Slate copy | `#53616C` | `--ink-soft` | Supporting text |
| Foundation navy | `#0F1E46` | `--navy` | Parent-brand footer, statistic tiles, hero route bar |
| Construction navy | `#082B54` | `--construction-navy` | Hero's three-building silhouette, sampled from the Construction logo |
| Construction blue | `#014CBB` | `--construction` | Construction route and primary links |
| Construction sky | `#038DF3` | `--construction-bright` | Bright blue accent and keyboard focus |
| Developments teal | `#016B7B` | `--properties` | Developments route and map marker |
| Developments bright teal | `#05C7CF` | `--properties-bright` | Bright end of the route bar |

Everett uses `#0F4F21` on its company-row hover and `#146B29` for its map markers and legend. The supplied Everett logo keeps its own embedded colours. Do not spread Everett green across the Group hero or footer.

The thin bar between the hero and company list runs Construction blue to sky, Foundation navy, then Developments teal to bright teal. The rest of the hero stays mineral grey; there is no liquid gradient or background U.

Check the rendered result for sufficient contrast when placing text on any company colour. Use the supplied logo artwork as-is instead of recreating its embedded gradients in CSS.
