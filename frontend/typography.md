# Typography

The Group site pairs **Space Grotesk** for headings and brand-sized labels with **Archivo** for body text and navigation. Both are loaded through `next/font/google` in [`app/layout.tsx`](../app/layout.tsx), with weights 400, 500, 600, and 700 available. CSS uses `--font-heading` and `--font-body` in [`app/globals.css`](../app/globals.css).

Space Grotesk gives the large editorial headings their shape. Archivo remains readable in company descriptions and map-adjacent copy. Keep this pairing on Group materials; the separate company sites may have their own typography.

| Element | Family | Current treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero statement | Space Grotesk | Weight 500, responsive `3.5rem` to `5.5rem`, tight line height |
| Section heading | Space Grotesk | Weight 500, responsive sizing |
| Company name | Space Grotesk | Weight 500, large sentence-case heading |
| Body and navigation | Archivo | Mostly weight 400 or 500, standard reading line height |
| Small labels and actions | Archivo | Weight 600, restrained letter spacing |

Use sentence case for Group headings and controls. Avoid repeated all-caps construction language, heavy display gradients, and lettering that mimics technical drawings. For new copy, keep paragraphs narrow enough to read comfortably on desktop and allow natural wrapping on phones.
